# Video Hook

Write the first 3 seconds of a short video so viewers stop scrolling. A hook is a promise, a tension, or a curiosity gap that the rest of the video must pay off. Never write a hook the video cannot deliver.

## Inputs to collect first

- Topic or product (required). If the user only gives a product name, ask what it does or what is unusual about it.
- Platform and target viewer, if known. Default: TikTok, Indonesian audience.
- Language: default Bahasa Indonesia casual. Follow the user if they ask for English or mixed.

## Workflow

1. Identify the strongest raw material in the topic: a surprising result, a mistake people make, a before/after contrast, a number, an enemy (a bad habit, a rip-off, a myth), or an unfinished story.
2. Generate 12 hooks spread across at least 4 pattern families below. Do not generate 12 variations of the same sentence.
3. Rank the top 3 and say, in one line each, why they work and what the video must show to pay them off.

## Pattern families

- **Result first**: show the outcome, then rewind. "7 hari doang, follower naik 11 ribu. Ini yang gw lakuin."
- **Mistake callout**: name the error the viewer is probably making. "Berhenti posting jam segini, itu yang bikin views lu mati."
- **Curiosity gap**: open a loop, close it late. "Ada satu setting yang hampir ga ada yang nyentuh, padahal ngaruh banget."
- **Contrast**: expectation vs reality. "Kirain butuh kamera mahal. Ternyata HP kentang juga bisa, asalkan..."
- **Direct challenge**: speak to one specific viewer. "Buat lu yang jualan di Shopee tapi videonya sepi, dengerin."
- **Numbered promise**: a concrete count and payoff. "3 kesalahan edit yang bikin video lu diskip."

## Rules

- One hook = one spoken line or one on-screen text, max about 12 words. If it needs a comma splice to make sense, split or cut it.
- The first 3 words carry the weight. Put the number, the result, or the loaded word early.
- No clickbait the video cannot pay off, no fake claims, no invented statistics. If a number is used, it must come from the user or the video content.
- Avoid generic motivational filler ("rahasia sukses yang tidak pernah diberitahu"). Abstract AI-sounding diction is a failure mode; concrete details win.
- Match register to the audience: casual lu/gw for Indonesian youth audiences, polite for professional topics. Never force slang.

## Output format

A ranked list of 12 hooks grouped by pattern family, then a "Top 3" section with the payoff note for each. Keep commentary out of the hook list itself.
