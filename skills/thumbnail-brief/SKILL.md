# Thumbnail Brief

A thumbnail is a promise the viewer can read in half a second, at phone size, inside a crowded feed. It is not a poster and it is not a screenshot with random text on it. Every brief must survive being shrunk to the size of a postage stamp: if the main subject and the main words are not obvious at that size, the brief fails.

## Inputs to collect first

- What the video shows (required): topic or product, the key moment, the payoff.
- The hook line, if one exists. If the user has no hook yet, write one first using the same rule as the video's first 3 seconds: one tension, one promise, nothing the video cannot pay off.
- Platform (required if known). Default: TikTok, 9:16.
- Face or no face: does the creator appear, is it a product only video, or is it a faceless format. Never assume a face is available.
- Any frame or product photo the user can supply. A real frame from the actual video beats a generated lookalike for product accuracy.

## Workflow

1. Pick the single strongest visual: the product in use, the result, the before and after contrast, or the moment of surprise. One subject only. If two subjects compete, cut one.
2. Write the thumbnail text from the hook, then cut it to the word limit below. The text adds tension; it does not repeat the title or caption.
3. Build the composition: subject position, text position, contrast plan, and what must stay inside the safe zones for each platform.
4. Write variant B as a real alternative, not a recolor: change one variable only (different text, different subject framing, or face versus faceless) so the test teaches something.
5. Write the deliverables section: a designer brief a human can execute without asking questions, and an AI image prompt a generator can execute without inventing product details.

## Text rules

- Max 3 to 4 words on the thumbnail. Hard limit. If it needs a fifth word, the idea is not sharp enough yet.
- Words must be readable at feed size: short words, no thin fonts, no full sentences, no punctuation clutter.
- Use the loaded word early: a number, a result, or a warning word ("Stop", "Salah", "Gratis" only when true) carries the first line.
- Never put claims, prices, or discounts on a thumbnail that the video or listing does not prove.
- Language follows the video: default Bahasa Indonesia casual for Indonesian audiences. Keep text in one language, do not mix.

## Composition rules

- Product close up first for selling videos: the product fills at least a third of the frame, shown at the angle it is actually used, with the variant color or model exactly matching the video and the listing.
- Contrast is mandatory: subject separated from background by brightness, color, or outline. Busy backgrounds are banned behind text.
- Face option: if a face is used, one face, large, with one clear expression that matches the hook (surprise, doubt, relief). No neutral passport expressions, no crowd of faces.
- Faceless option: crop at the neck or shoot hands and product only. This is often the stronger choice for affiliate product videos because the product stays the hero and the format stays consistent across a whole series.
- Leave a clean text zone: top third or left third, decided before the designer starts, never wherever space happens to remain.
- Nothing important at the very bottom: platform captions, progress bars, and UI buttons cover it.

## Per platform sizes

Deliver the master at 1080 x 1920 (9:16) and note the crop risk per platform:

- **TikTok**: 1080 x 1920. Keep text and product inside the center area; the right side carries the action buttons and the bottom carries the caption.
- **Instagram Reels**: master 1080 x 1920, but the profile grid crops covers toward a square and the feed preview crops toward 4:5. Keep all text and the product inside the central 1080 x 1350 zone so no crop can cut a word.
- **Facebook Reels**: 1080 x 1920. Audiences skew older, so prefer the upper end of text size and the plainest wording.
- **YouTube Shorts**: the frame itself is the thumbnail in most surfaces, at 1080 x 1920. The chosen frame must already contain the contrast and subject size; do not rely on a custom cover being shown everywhere. If the same video is also uploaded as a regular video, add a separate 1280 x 720 (16:9) version of the brief.
- **Shopee Video**: 1080 x 1920 cover or first frame. Product accuracy rules are strictest here: the cover product must be the exact variant sold, no decoration that looks like an extra item included in the price.

## Do and do not

Do:
- Test at small size before approving a design.
- Keep one visual system across a series (same text position, same outline style) so viewers learn to recognize the channel.
- Match the thumbnail promise to the first 3 seconds of the video.

Do not:
- Do not use a random stock style image that shows a different product model or color than the video.
- Do not stack logos, stickers, arrows, and circles together. One graphic accent max.
- Do not use red arrows and shocked faces by default. They are a pattern, not a law, and they clash with plain product review formats.
- Do not put text over the product itself or over a face.
- Do not promise a result, a discount, or a before and after that the video does not show.

## Rules

- One brief, one subject, one idea. A thumbnail that needs an explanation has already failed.
- Product accuracy outranks attractiveness: wrong variant, wrong color, or extra accessories in frame are grounds to redo the brief.
- If the user only has a raw frame and no design budget, say so in the brief and optimize frame choice and text overlay instead of inventing a design that will never be made.
- Never invent statistics or results for the text. Numbers come from the user or the video.

## Output format

Deliver in this order:

1. **Main brief**: platform and size, source frame or subject description, exact thumbnail text (in quotes), text position, subject position and scale, contrast plan, safe zone notes.
2. **Variant B brief**: same sections, with the one changed variable named in the first line.
3. **Designer checklist**: a short list the designer confirms before exporting (variant correct, text spelled right, safe zones respected, small size test passed).
4. **AI image prompt**: one paste-ready prompt describing the subject, framing, lighting, and background, with the exact product details from the video, and a note that final text is added in the editor, not generated inside the image.
